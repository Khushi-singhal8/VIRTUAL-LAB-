To study and operate micrometer Screw Gauge for dimensional measurement

Theory

A micrometer screw gauge is a precision measuring instrument used to measure very small dimensions...

Each full rotation of the thimble moves the spindle by a fixed distance called the pitch...

LC = 0.5 mm / 25 = 0.02 mm

VLC = 0.02 / 10 = 0.002 mm

Main parts: U-frame, anvil, spindle, sleeve, thimble, ratchet stop, lock lever.

Applications:

  • Measuring wires
  • Measuring sheet thickness
  • Precision components

Advantages:

  • High accuracy
  • Easy to use
  • Portable

Disadvantages:

  • Measures small dimensions only
  • More expensive
  • Requires careful handling
