To study butt joint welding using gas oxy-acetylene method

Procedure

Step 1: Cleaning of first plate edge

Drag the file tool along the bevelled edge of the first plate to remove rust, dirt, and oxide layers.

Step 2: First plate edge cleaned

The bevel edge of the first plate is now clean and ready for welding.

Step 3: Preparation for second plate cleaning

Place the file tool at the highlighted area on the bevelled edge of the second plate.

Step 4: Cleaning of second plate edge

Drag the file tool along the bevel edge to clean the second plate surface.

Step 5: Aligning first plate

Click on the red marked area to slide and position the first plate on the worktable.

Step 6: Aligning second plate

Click on the red marked area to align the second plate and form a proper V-groove.

Step 7: Setting acetylene gas pressure

Adjust the acetylene regulator until the pressure gauge reads 10 psi.

Step 8: Setting oxygen gas pressure

Adjust the oxygen regulator until the pressure gauge reads 25 psi.

Step 9: Opening acetylene valve

Slowly open the acetylene valve on the torch handle to allow gas flow.

Step 10: Ignition of flame

Use a spark lighter to ignite the acetylene gas and produce a yellow flame.

Step 11: Adjustment to neutral flame

Open the fuel gas valve slightly and ignite the gas using a spark lighter to produce an initial flame.

Step 12: Add oxygen to the flame

Gradually open the oxygen control valve to adjust the flame characteristics.

Step 13: Achieve neutral flame

Continue adjusting the oxygen valve until a stable blue neutral flame is obtained, indicating proper gas balance.

Step 14: Positioning for first tack weld

Position the torch at the highlighted location for the first tack weld.

Step 15: First tack welding

Apply heat until molten metal forms a tack weld at one end of the joint.

Step 16: Second tack weld positioning

Move the torch to the opposite end of the joint.

Step 17: Second tack welding

Form the second tack weld to firmly hold both plates.

Step 18: Performing continuous welding

Move the torch steadily along the V-groove to produce a uniform weld bead.

Step 19: Weld bead completion

The weld bead is completed and covered with slag.

Step 20: Slag removal

Use a chipping hammer to gently remove slag from the weld.

Step 21: Dressing of weld bead

File the weld bead to improve surface finish.
