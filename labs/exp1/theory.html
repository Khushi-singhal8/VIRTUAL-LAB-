To study and operate vernier calliper for dimensional measurement

Theory

The Vernier caliper is a common precision instrument used in workshops and laboratories...

Main parts of a Vernier caliper include:

  • Main scale engraved on the beam.
  • Vernier scale that slides along the main scale.
  • Outer jaws for measuring external diameters.
  • Inner jaws for internal diameters.
  • Depth rod for measuring depth.
