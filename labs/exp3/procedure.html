To prepare the simple wooden pattern and study the basic pattern making process

Procedure

This simulation demonstrates the turning operation on a lathe machine, including setup, machining, finishing, and measurement steps.

  1. These are the different types of wood used in pattern making:
    • Select a suitable wood block to be used as the workpiece.
    • Ensure the material is of appropriate size and free from defects.
  2. Mount Workpiece in the Chuck:
    • Place the wooden workpiece securely in the lathe chuck.
    • Tighten the jaws properly to hold the workpiece firmly.
  3. Move Tool Post by Engaging Lever:
    • Engage the lever to bring the tool post into position.
    • Ensure proper alignment for machining.
  4. Stabilize Workpiece Using Tailstock:
    • Slide the tailstock towards the workpiece.
    • Lock it in place to provide additional support and stability.
  5. Mount Tool on Tool Post:
    • Drag and drop the cutting tool into the tool post in the simulation.
    • Fix the tool firmly for smooth cutting operation.
  6. Start the Operation:
    • Press the green start button on the lathe control panel.
    • The chuck rotates the workpiece for machining.
  7. Finishing Process:
    • After machining, apply sandpaper on the rotating workpiece.
    • Smoothen the surface for a fine finish.
  8. Check Diameter with Vernier Caliper:
    • Use a Vernier caliper to measure the finished diameter.
    • Ensure the measurement is accurate within tolerance limits.
  9. Check length with Ruler:
    • Use a ruler for approximate measurement of the workpiece length.
    • This provides a quick check for size verification.
