To study and operate vernier calliper for dimensional measurement

Procedure

Measure internal diameter of workpiece

  1. Unscrew the lock screw
  2. Move jaws using finger hook
  3. Move workpiece to correct position
  4. Move jaws using finger hook
  5. Tighten the lock screw
  6. Take reading
