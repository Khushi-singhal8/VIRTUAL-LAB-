To study and operate vernier calliper for dimensional measurement

Procedure

Outer Diameter Measurement

  1. Unlock lock screws – loosen the locking screw.
  2. Open outer jaws using the finger hook/roller.
  3. Place the workpiece between the two outer jaws.
  4. Close the jaws gently using the finger hook until they touch the workpiece.
  5. Tighten the lock screw to freeze the reading.
  6. Take the reading from Main scale + Vernier scale.

Fig (a): step1

Inner Diameter Measurement

  1. Unlock the lock screw.
  2. Open the inner jaws inside the hole.
  3. Gently expand the inner jaws until they touch the internal surface.
  4. Tighten the lock screw.
  5. Read: Main Scale + Vernier Scale.

Fig (b): step2

Depth Measurement

  1. Unlock the screw.
  2. Pull the depth rod out by sliding the scale.
  3. Place the caliper base on the top surface of the hole.
  4. Extend the depth rod until it touches the bottom surface.
  5. Tighten lock screw.
  6. Take the reading.

Fig (c): step3

Thickness Measurement

    (Thickness = difference between two parallel surfaces)
  1. Use outer jaws.
  2. Place jaws on the two faces whose thickness you need.
  3. Close jaws until they touch.
  4. Lock screw.
  5. Read the measurement.

Fig (d): step4
