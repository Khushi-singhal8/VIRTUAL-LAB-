To study butt joint welding with V-groove using (MIG) welding.

Procedure

Step 1: Select First Plate

Click on the highlighted area on the first plate to begin alignment.

Step 2: Select Second Plate

Click on the highlighted area on the second plate to align both plates together.

Step 3: Surface Cleaning

Drag the wire brush over the plate surface to remove rust and impurities.

Step 4: Set Welding voltage

Adjust the welding voltage to the required value using the power source control.

Step 5: Set the wire feed rate

Adjust the wire feed rate to 88 mm/s using the power source control.

Step 6: Adjust Gas Flow

Set the shielding gas flow rate to 10 LPM for proper arc protection.

Step 7: Start Welding

Drag the welding torch along the joint area to start welding the plates.

Step 8: Complete Welding

Continue moving the torch along the joint until the weld bead is formed completely.

Step 9: Slag Removal

Drag the cleaning tool over the welded joint to remove slag and residues.
