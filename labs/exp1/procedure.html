To study and operate vernier calliper for dimensional measurement

Procedure

Internal Diameter Measurement

Step 1(i): Unscrew lock screw

Unscrew the lock screw to allow free movement of the sliding jaw.

Step 1(ii): Move jaws

Move the jaws using the finger hook to create space for the workpiece.

Step 1(iii): Position workpiece

Insert the workpiece between the inner jaws at the correct position.

Step 1(iv): Adjust jaws

Move the jaws carefully until they touch the internal surface of the workpiece.

Step 1(v): Tighten lock screw

Fix the jaw position by tightening the lock screw.

Step 1(vi): Take reading

Observe the main scale and vernier scale and record the measurement.

Outer Diameter Measurement

Step 2(i): Unscrew lock screw

Loosen the lock screw to move the outer jaws freely.

Step 2(ii): Open jaws

Slide the outer jaws using the finger hook.

Step 2(iii): Place workpiece

Position the workpiece between the outer measuring jaws.

Step 2(iv): Adjust jaws

Move jaws until they gently touch both sides of the workpiece.

Step 2(v): Tighten lock screw

Secure the position by tightening the lock screw.

Step 2(vi): Take reading

Read and note the outer diameter from the scales.

Thickness Measurement

Step 3(i): Unscrew lock screw

Loosen the lock screw to allow jaw movement.

Step 3(ii): Move jaws

Open the jaws using the finger hook.

Step 3(iii): Position workpiece

Place the workpiece between the jaws.

Step 3(iv): Adjust jaws

Bring the jaws into light contact with the surface.

Step 3(v): Tighten lock screw

Lock the jaw position firmly.

Step 3(vi): Take reading

Record the thickness measurement from the scale.

Depth Measurement

Step 4(i): Position workpiece

Place the workpiece correctly on the reference surface.

Step 4(ii): Unscrew lock screw

Loosen the lock screw to allow depth rod movement.

Step 4(iii): Extend depth rod

Slide the depth rod until it touches the bottom of the cavity.

Step 4(iv): Tighten lock screw

Lock the rod in position.

Step 4(v): Take reading

Observe and note the depth measurement.
