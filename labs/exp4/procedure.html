To study the bending process of a metal specimen and evaluate spring back effect.

Procedure

Step 1: Material and thickness selection

Select the required material (Aluminium, Brass, or Stainless Steel) and choose the thickness (2 mm or 5 mm). The selection is confirmed through the green status bar.

Step 2: Viewing the schematic diagram

The schematic top and side view of the metal strip is displayed to understand the geometry and dimensions.

Step 3: Marking the center of the specimen

Measure equal distances of 60 mm from both ends and mark the midpoint for bending reference.

Step 4(i): Placing the workpiece on the die

Drag the workpiece into the highlighted yellow dotted region on the V-shaped lower die.

Step 4(ii): Workpiece positioned correctly

The workpiece lies across the die within the green area and the message “Placed correctly!” appears.

Step 5(i): Attaching the handle to the screw

Drag the handle and align it with the screw head inside the yellow dotted region.

Step 5(ii): Handle attached successfully

The handle snaps into position forming the complete lever mechanism. The confirmation message appears.

Step 6(i): Starting the bending operation

Click the highlighted red area on the handle to initiate the downward movement of the punch.

Step 6(ii): Measuring bend angle before release

Align the protractor with the bent workpiece to measure the angle while the punch is still applying force.

Step 6(iii): Final bend angle and spring-back

After releasing the load, measure the relaxed angle using the protractor to observe the spring-back effect.

Step 7(i): Removing the punch

Click the handle again to move the punch upward and remove the applied force.

Step 7(ii): Measuring bend angle after punch removal

Align the protractor to measure the bend angle immediately after unloading.

Step 7(iii): Measuring final bend angle (spring-back)

Allow the sheet to fully relax and measure the final angle to evaluate the spring-back effect.
